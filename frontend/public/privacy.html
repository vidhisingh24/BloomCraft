Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

BloomCraft (bloomcraftt.vercel.app) is a small handmade crochet studio in Vadodara, Gujarat, India, run by its founder Vidhi Singh. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and e-mail address when you create an account, and your mobile number if you add it to your profile.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, e-mail address and profile picture with us. We do not receive your Google password or access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Orders: the items you buy, your delivery details (address, college or handover area), phone number, gift message, and for UPI payments the 12-digit UPI reference number (UTR) you enter.
  • Saved addresses you choose to keep in your profile.
  • Custom-order requests: your name, phone number and the idea you describe.

We never collect or store bank details, card numbers or UPI PINs. UPI payments go directly from your UPI app to the maker’s UPI account.

2. How we use your information

  • To make, deliver and hand over your order, and to contact you about it (for example on WhatsApp).
  • To let you sign in, see your order history and reuse saved addresses.
  • To confirm that a UPI payment was received.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of the website.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell, rent or trade it to anyone.

3. Who can see it

Only you and BloomCraft. Your data is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India), and the website is hosted by Vercel. Access rules in the database make sure each customer can only see their own orders, addresses and profile. If you message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, those apps’ own privacy policies apply to those conversations.

4. Google user data

Information received from Google (name, e-mail address, profile picture) is used only to create and sign in to your BloomCraft account and to show your name on the website. It is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not transferred except as required to provide this service. BloomCraft’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. On your device

Your cart, wishlist and an unfinished checkout are kept in your own browser so they are not lost when you close the page. Your sign-in session is also stored in the browser until you sign out. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep it

Account and address details are kept while your account exists. Order records are kept as long as needed for delivery, returns and accounting.

7. Your choices

You can update your name, phone number and addresses at any time from My Profile. To see, correct or delete your data or your whole account, use Settings → Delete account on the website or message us on WhatsApp at +91 93160 97667. We respond within 7 days.

8. Children

BloomCraft is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
